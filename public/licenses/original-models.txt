The chicken, chick and rabbit rigs in public/models/*.glb and the feed
illustrations in public/branding/feed-*.svg and foody-mark.svg were drawn
for this Expo Vet demo. They are not third-party downloads.

Quaternius and Kenney publish CC0 animals, but a chicken + chick + rabbit
set with idle/walk/eat clips could not be fetched reliably while preparing
this build (the published farm packs on hand do not include that trio, and
the Poly Pizza files were behind a bot check). These original low-poly rigs
replace the earlier sphere placeholders. Swap any of them by replacing the
GLB at the same path; the game looks for Body, Torso, Head, WingL, WingR,
LegL, LegR and, on the rabbit, EarL and EarR.

The Foody wordmark and colors here are stand-in art until the official
brand kit is dropped in. See README.md, “Swap the brand”.
